PhotoVeil Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Back to PhotoVeil support page

Data collection

PhotoVeil does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The app requires no account, contains no analytics or advertising frameworks, and does not track you. It accesses photos only through the iOS system photo picker, which gives the app access solely to the specific images you select — the app never requests access to your full photo library.

Processing

All image processing — EXIF and metadata inspection, metadata removal, and purpose watermarking — happens entirely on your device. Cleaned and watermarked exports are written to your device's local storage. No image and no metadata ever leaves your device or is uploaded to an external server.

Third parties

PhotoVeil integrates no third-party SDKs or services that collect or process user data. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised text will be published on this page with an updated date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact link on the support page.

PhotoVeil 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 10 月 11 日

数据收集

PhotoVeil 不在任何服务器上收集、传输或存储个人信息。本应用无需注册账号，不含数据分析或广告组件，不追踪用户。应用仅通过 iOS 系统照片选择器读取照片，只能访问您主动选中的图片，绝不索取完整相册的访问权限。

数据处理

所有处理——EXIF 与元数据查看、元数据抹除、用途水印——均在您的设备本地完成。导出图片直接保存在本机，任何图片或元数据都不会离开设备、不会上传到外部服务器。

第三方服务

本应用未集成任何收集或处理用户数据的第三方 SDK 或服务，不与任何第三方共享或出售数据。

政策变更

本政策如有修订，将在本页面发布并更新上方日期。
