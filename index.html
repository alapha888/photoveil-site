PhotoVeil — support

PhotoVeil is an iOS app in development that shows you what a photo reveals about you — GPS location, camera and device details, capture time — and removes that metadata before you share the photo. It can also tile a purpose line such as “For rental application only” across copies of ID cards, contracts, or receipts.

How it works

  • EXIF report: location, device, time, software and lens info, with sensitive items flagged.
  • One-tap clean export: JPEG metadata is removed losslessly (image data copied byte for byte); other formats are re-encoded without metadata.
  • Purpose watermark: watermarked exports are metadata-free too.
  • Private by design: every step runs on your device. No uploads, no network requests, no analytics, no accounts. The app uses the system photo picker and never asks for full photo-library access.

Note: cleaning removes embedded metadata; it cannot remove information that is visible in the image itself.
